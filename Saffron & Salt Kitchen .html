About us

A small kitchen that grew from a family table.

Saffron & Salt started in 2014 when Maria Reyes began cooking extra portions of her grandmother’s recipes for neighbors. The demand grew faster than the kitchen, so she opened a small shop with two burners and a long counter.

Today a team of twelve cooks every day with the same rule: if we wouldn’t serve it at our own table, it doesn’t go out. We use fresh ingredients, season carefully and never rush a braise.

We also send unsold meals to local shelters each evening, so nothing good goes to waste.
